## Why Nousivo?

Every Nousivo app is built on three promises:

- **🔒 Private by design** — your data stays on your device. No Nousivo servers, no analytics, no tracking. **Data Not Collected.**
- **🔑 Bring Your Own Key** — use the AI provider *you* choose: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or fully local models via Ollama / LM Studio.
- **⚡ Works offline** — on-device Apple Intelligence means real AI answers even with no network and no API key.

| | Big Tech Apps | Nousivo Apps |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Your data | Uploaded to the cloud | Stays on your device |
| AI provider | Locked to one | 9+ providers — your choice, your key |
| Works offline | ❌ | ✅ On-device AI included |
| Accounts required | ✅ | ❌ No sign-up, ever |

## The Suite

Five focused apps. One private AI core. Every app features an AI chief of staff that plans, drafts and suggests — and **always asks before it acts**.

### 📄 SeekerPDF — AI PDF Intelligence
*Read, ask, annotate, translate and automate any document.*

- Camera-to-PDF scanning with on-device OCR
- Chat with documents — answers with **page-level citations**
- AI summaries, translations, deep research analysis
- Natural-language workflow automation for documents

### 📚 StudyG — AI Study Companion
*Learn faster with AI-powered flashcards, quizzes and tutoring.*

- Flashcards with FSRS spaced repetition and adaptive quizzes
- AI tutor: Socratic dialogue, essay coaching, homework hints
- Study planner, focus sessions, streaks and exam countdowns
- Apple Watch app, widgets and Live Activities

### 💼 SI Career Coach — AI Job Search & Career Coach
*Find roles that fit, tailor every application, walk in prepared.*

- Company Watch tracks careers pages; ATS Match scores your resume
- Resume builder (12 professional styles) and tailored cover letters
- Application pipeline with follow-up radar and interview prep
- Offer comparison and negotiation coaching

### 🗂️ SI Organizer — AI Planner, Tasks & Habits
*A private chief of staff for your whole life.*

- Energy-aware daily planning around your calendar
- Tasks, habits, bills, subscriptions and renewal reminders — one place
- Optional Apple Health integration (read-only, stays on device)
- Brain Dump, chores, shared lists and Life Score

### 🏢 SI WorkHub — AI Meetings, Docs & Projects
*Your entire workday, organized and searchable.*

- Meeting prep briefs, recording, transcription and AI minutes
- Read, compare and redline documents and contracts with citations
- Time tracking, invoicing, projects and a contacts CRM
- Weekly reports and workflow automation

> The SI apps work together: send a task from **WorkHub** to **Organizer** or **Career Coach** in one tap.
